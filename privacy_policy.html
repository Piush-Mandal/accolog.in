Privacy Policy

Accolog — Account Log, Simplified Effective date: September 3, 2026  •  Last updated: September 3, 2026

Accolog is a lightweight account-tracking tool built for bank staff to privately log the accounts they personally open, for their own follow-up and record-keeping. This policy explains what limited information the app collects, how it is stored, and the responsibilities that come with using it. Please read this alongside your employer's own data-handling policies, which always take precedence.

1

Who This App Is For

Accolog is intended solely for individual bank employees (relationship managers, DSAs, branch staff, etc.) to maintain a personal log of accounts they have opened during the course of their work. It is not an official banking product, is not affiliated with or endorsed by any bank, and is not intended for customers of a bank to use directly.

Each user of Accolog is responsible for ensuring their use of this app complies with their employer's internal data protection, confidentiality, and IT security policies. If your workplace prohibits storing customer information in third-party or personal apps, please do not use Accolog for that purpose.

2

Information We Collect

Accolog is designed to collect the minimum information necessary for it to function:

  • Account credentials: your email address and password (or Google account identifier, if you sign in with Google), used solely to authenticate you.
  • Records you choose to enter: the customer name and account number for each account you log. Accolog does not request or store balances, transaction history, KYC documents, contact details, or any other customer information beyond these two fields.
  • Basic profile details you optionally provide, such as your name or branch, to personalise your own dashboard.

Accolog does not access your device's contacts, camera, location, or files, and does not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

3

How We Use This Information

Information entered into Accolog is used only to:

  • Display your own logged records back to you within the app.
  • Authenticate you securely on each login.
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the underlying database.

We do not use any information you enter for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose beyond letting you view and manage your own records.

4

Data Storage & Security

Records are stored using Firebase Realtime Database and secured with Firebase Authentication. Access rules are configured so that each worker can only read and write their own records — no other user of the app, including other bank workers, can view your logged accounts.

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections.
  • Database access is authentication-gated; anonymous or public access is disabled.
  • We recommend enabling any in-app screen lock or PIN feature for an additional layer of protection on your device.
No storage system is 100% immune to risk. While reasonable technical safeguards are in place, you should avoid logging any information beyond what Accolog explicitly asks for (name and account number), and should follow your employer's guidance on acceptable data handling at all times.
5

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share the information you enter into Accolog with any third party, advertiser, or data broker. Data may only be disclosed:

  • When required to comply with a valid legal process, court order, or government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Accolog, its users, or the public in cases of suspected fraud or misuse.
6

Data Retention

Your records are retained for as long as your account remains active. If you delete an individual record, it is permanently removed from the database. If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated records, contact us using the details below and we will process the request within a reasonable timeframe.

7

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information associated with your account. Since Accolog is built around a simple per-user structure, you can:

  • View, edit, or delete any record you've logged directly within the app at any time.
  • Request full deletion of your account and all data by contacting us.
8

Children's Privacy

Accolog is a workplace tool intended for adult bank employees and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
